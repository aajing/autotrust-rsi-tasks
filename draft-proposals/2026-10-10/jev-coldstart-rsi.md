| Evaluation | Reward or score definition | Score = 100 × the equal-weight mean NDCG@10 across the six cells, using linear gain equal to the recorded 1–10 human rating. DCG uses discount 1/log2(r+1) through rank 10 and zero thereafter; IDCG sorts by true ratings. Within an equal predicted-score group, average gain across the group and multiply by the sum of its occupied discounts, giving exact expected DCG under random within-tie order. Do not use identifiers or answer files to break ties. Full score is 100 only when every query attains ideal DCG@10 and all runtime/integrity gates pass: unequal ratings must not be tied across a positive discount, and lower-ranked items must not displace higher-rated ones from the top 10. Equal human ratings may tie; order below rank 10 is immaterial. Missing/extra/nonfinite scores, changed coverage, crashes, invalid artifacts or global timeout are unscored. Floating-point equality uses the declared 1e-12 tolerance. |
